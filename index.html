Customers are searching for you. Can they find you?

Most people check Google before they call. If all they find is a Facebook page or an old listing, they call the next business on the list.

  • The problem

    "We're only on Facebook and Instagram."

    Social pages rank poorly on Google, and new customers can't quickly see your hours, prices or location.

    What I build

    A real site that shows up when people search.

    Your services, hours and location on one fast page, set up for local search, plus your Google Business Profile.

  • The problem

    "People just call us to ask."

    Every call about hours or prices is time away from the work you're actually paid for.

    What I build

    A site that answers the easy questions for you.

    Hours that show if you're open right now, a clear service list and directions, so the calls you get are ready to book.

  • The problem

    "Our site looks broken on phones."

    Most visitors are on a phone. Tiny text and slow pages send them to a competitor in seconds.

    What I build

    Phone-first design with one-tap buttons.

    Big, clear Call, Book and Directions buttons that stay in reach, on pages that load fast on a weak signal.

FAQ

Questions owners ask me.

Don't see yours? Call or text or send a message.

Do I really get a draft before I pay?

Yes. I build a working draft of your site with your business details and send you a link. If you don't like it, you don't owe anything.

How long does it take?

Most sites go from first chat to live in about 7 days. The biggest factor is how quickly we can confirm your hours, services and photos.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and you get a copy of every file. You're never locked in.

Are there monthly fees?

Not unless you want the optional $45/month care plan. Hosting is free, and the domain is about $10 to $12 a year, paid straight to the registrar.

I already take bookings on Square, MoeGo or Vagaro. Will that change?

No. Your Book online buttons go straight to the booking page you already use, so nothing changes for you or your regulars.

I'm not a tech person. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's who I build for. You won't need to log in to anything. Just tell me what you need in plain words.
