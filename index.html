Questions before you start

Straight answers.

Do I really get a draft before I pay?

Yes. No deposit and no card needed. I build a working draft of your site with your business details and send you a link. If it's not right for you, just say so. You don't owe anything.

What's the founding-client deal?

My first 3 website clients get $100 off: Starter is $250 instead of $350, and Business is $850 instead of $950. You see a free draft first. After launch, I may ask for honest feedback and permission to feature your site. The discount does not depend on a review. Once the 3 spots are taken, the offer ends.

How long does the free draft take, and what do you need from me?

Usually 2–3 days after we first talk. I just need your business name, services, hours, phone number and any logo or photos you have. A Facebook page or Google listing is often enough to start, and you can send everything by text.

What's the difference between the text-back and the booking agent?

The missed-call text-back ($297/month) texts every caller you miss within a minute, finds out what they need and sends their reply to you, so you call back a lead that's already warm. The booking agent ($1,500 setup + $1,000/month) goes further: it offers times that are really open and books the appointment into your calendar. Most contractors and shops only need the text-back. Offices that book appointments all day usually want the booking agent.

How does the missed-call text-back work?

When a connected business number misses a call from a text-capable number, an automatic text asks what the caller needs. Their reply reaches your lead inbox for follow-up. The message identifies itself as automated and provides an opt-out. I check your phone setup and explain number arrangements, usage allowances and any extra charges before you pay. The basic text-back does not book appointments for you.

Are there monthly fees or contracts?

One-time websites include static hosting. Domain renewal is paid to the registrar and varies by domain. The $45/month care plan is optional. Missed-call text-back is $297/month, website + text-back is $497/month plus $497 setup, and the booking agent is $1,500 setup plus $1,000/month. Monthly services are month to month. Paid email or booking subscriptions, usage allowances, any extra charges and cancellation arrangements are confirmed in writing before paid work starts.

What if I already have a website I don't like?

That's common. I'll keep what's working (your content, photos and web address) and build the free draft as a replacement. If you go ahead, I point your existing domain at the new site, so your address doesn't change. If an old web company controls your domain, I'll help you get it back into your name.

Can you use my existing logo, photos and content?

Yes. Send whatever you have: logo, photos, price list, menu or the text from your current site. If something's missing or blurry, I'll tell you and suggest options. The Business plan includes a custom logo if you need a new one.

How long until my site is live?

I reply in 1–3 business days. Your free draft is usually ready 2–3 days after I have your business details. We agree on the revision and launch schedule once the scope is clear; timing depends on your content and feedback.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name and you get a copy of every file. You're never locked in.

What happens if I need changes after the site is live?

Text me what you need. On the care plan and the monthly plans, updates like hours, prices and photos are included. Otherwise, I'll tell you the price before I do any work, so there are no surprise bills. And since you have every file, you're free to have anyone make changes.

I already take bookings on Square, MoeGo or Vagaro. Will that change?

No. Your Book online buttons go straight to the booking page you already use, so nothing changes for you or your regulars.

How does the missed-call booking agent work?

It replies within a minute to missed calls, finds out what the caller needs, holds an open slot, and books the appointment into your calendar after collecting their name and email. It always identifies itself as automated and includes a way to reach a person (reply STAFF). Pricing is $1,500 setup + $1,000/month, and I confirm it in writing before setup. It works with most practice calendars and does not require a new website. If yours can't connect, I'll tell you before you pay anything. Try the demo to see a full conversation.

I'm not a tech person. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's who I build for. You won't need to log in to anything. Just tell me what you need in plain words.

What happens if I cancel a monthly service?

Monthly plans are month to month. Your domain remains in your name and you receive your site files. Before you start, I confirm the cancellation steps, billing cutoff, what happens to automated replies and connected numbers, and any transfer work. Third-party subscriptions have their own terms.
