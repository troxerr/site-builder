Live demo · Missed-call booking agent

A missed call becomes a booked appointment in under a minute.

When the front desk can't pick up, the agent texts the caller back, says it’s an automated assistant, finds out what they need, offers real open times and books the one they choose. The front desk gets a clean summary. If anything is unclear or urgent, it hands off to a person instead of guessing.

Bright Smile Dental and Dr. Patel are made up for this demo. The conversation is a scripted replay, so nothing is sent or booked.

One clear job

It books the appointment. Everything else goes to a person.

This is the simple version that practices actually use: one job, a fast reply, and a human fallback. It books into the scheduling system the practice already uses, so nobody has to learn a new one.

What it handles

  • Texts back every missed call within a minute
  • Says up front that it’s automated
  • New or existing patient, visit type and preferred times
  • Offers only times that are really open
  • Gets the patient’s name and email, then books, confirms and sends a reminder

When it hands off

  • Pain, swelling, bleeding or anything urgent
  • Requests it can't match to a visit type
  • Billing, insurance and treatment questions
  • Any patient who asks for a person

What the practice gets

  • Fewer patients lost to voicemail
  • A clean summary for every booking
  • A full log of every conversation
  • A front desk that isn't chasing callbacks
