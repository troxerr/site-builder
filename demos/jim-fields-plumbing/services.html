Plumbing Repairs & Installations

Leaks, running toilets, dripping faucets, broken pipes, and new fixtures. We find the cause, explain the fix, and get it done right.

  • Leak detection and repair
  • Faucet, sink, and toilet repair or replacement
  • Pipe repair and replacement
  • Garbage disposals
  • Shut-off valves and supply lines
  • New fixture installation

Drain Cleaning

Slow or clogged sinks, tubs, showers, and toilets cleared out, plus main line backups that affect the whole house.

  • Kitchen and bathroom sink drains
  • Toilet clogs
  • Tub and shower drains
  • Main sewer line backups
  • Troubleshooting drains that keep clogging

Water Heater Repair & Installation

No hot water, not enough hot water, a leaking tank, or strange noises. We repair what makes sense to repair and replace what doesn't.

  • Gas and electric water heaters
  • Pilot, burner, and element problems
  • Leaking tanks and relief valves
  • Water heater replacement
  • Help choosing the right size

Heating System Repair & Installation

Furnace won't start, blowing cold air, or cycling on and off? We troubleshoot and repair heating systems and install new ones when it's time.

  • Gas and electric furnaces
  • Heat pumps
  • Thermostat problems
  • Ignition and blower issues
  • New heating system installation

Air Conditioning Repair & Installation

A Memphis-area summer is no time to be without A/C. We diagnose and repair central air systems and install replacements.

  • A/C not cooling or blowing warm air
  • Frozen coils and drain line issues
  • Outdoor unit and condenser problems
  • Refrigerant issues
  • A/C replacement and installation

HVAC Maintenance

Seasonal tune-ups help your system run efficiently and catch small problems before they turn into breakdowns.

  • Spring A/C check-ups
  • Fall heating check-ups
  • Filter and airflow checks
  • Safety checks on gas equipment
  • Condensate drain line cleaning

General Residential Plumbing & HVAC

Not sure what to call the problem? Call and describe it. If it involves water, pipes, heating, or cooling in your home, we can most likely help.

  • Troubleshooting and diagnosis
  • Small jobs and odd repairs
  • Upgrades and replacements
  • Straight advice on repair vs. replace
