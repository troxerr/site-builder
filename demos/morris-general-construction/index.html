Questions

Questions we hear every week

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, call and ask.

Something broken right now?

Leak, storm damage or a door that won't lock? Call or text us a photo. We'll tell you what to do first and get you on the schedule.

How much will my remodel cost?

It depends on the size of the space, what's behind the walls and the finishes you pick, which is why we give you a written, itemized price after a free visit instead of a guess over the phone. You'll see labor, materials and allowances line by line, so you can adjust the scope to fit your budget.

Do I need a permit?

For anything structural, electrical, plumbing or an addition, usually yes. Memphis and unincorporated Shelby County permits go through Shelby County Code Enforcement, and suburbs like Bartlett, Germantown and Collierville have their own codes offices. We pull the permits and schedule the inspections.

How long will it take?

A bathroom typically takes two to four weeks once materials are in, and a kitchen four to eight. You'll get a schedule with your quote, and we'll tell you right away if a delivery or an inspection moves it.

Can we stay in the house during the work?

Usually, yes. We set up dust barriers and floor protection, clean up every day, and plan the work so you keep a working bathroom or kitchen whenever we can.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. We document the damage with dated photos, work from your adjuster's scope, and send itemized documentation if something was missed. You stay in charge of your claim, and you pay your deductible.

How do payments work?

A deposit to schedule and order materials, then payments tied to finished stages of the work, with the final payment after your walkthrough. It's all written into your contract before we start.

What warranty do I get?

Manufacturer warranties on the products we install, plus our own written workmanship warranty. Both are spelled out in your contract.
