Your first visit

What to expect, step by step.

New to us? Here's exactly how a visit goes, so there are no surprises for you or your pet.

  1. Call or request

    Tell us about your pet. We'll find a time and answer questions about cost.

  2. Check in

    Park in our free lot. Dogs on a leash, cats in a carrier, please.

  3. An unhurried exam

    Dr. Conley checks your pet nose to tail and walks you through it.

  4. A clear plan

    Options and prices before any treatment. Nothing happens without your okay.

  5. Follow-up

    Questions at home? Call us. We'd rather hear from you than have you worry.

What to bring

  • Past vaccine and vet records, if you have them
  • A list of any medicines or supplements
  • A leash for dogs, a carrier for cats
  • Notes on changes you've noticed, like eating or energy

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Still wondering? Give us a call.

Do you see exotic pets?

No. We focus on dogs and cats only, so all of our training, equipment and time goes to them.

Do I need an appointment?

Please call ahead so we can make sure there's time to see your pet without a long wait.

How much does a visit cost?

Prices depend on what your pet needs. Call us and we'll explain the cost before we do anything.

Where do I park?

We have a free parking lot at 4450 Summer Ave, between Perkins and Waring, next to Burger King. The building is wheelchair accessible.

What if my pet has an emergency after hours?

Go to the nearest 24-hour emergency animal hospital right away. Call us the next day we're open so we can follow up.
