Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

When a chair is open, yes. Call or text first and we'll tell you the wait.

How long does color take?

Most color takes 2 to 3 hours. Each service on the menu lists its time so you can plan your day.

Can I bring my kids?

Of course. We keep a small corner with books and crayons, and kids' cuts are $24.

What if I don't love it?

Tell us within a week and we'll fix it at no charge.
