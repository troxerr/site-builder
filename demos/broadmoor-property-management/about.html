How it started

Founded in 2003 by Kathy Lambert

Broadmoor started in 2003 with Kathy Lambert and a few rental units in Memphis. The idea was simple: treat owners' properties like an investment, treat residents like neighbors, and always pick up the phone.

Word got around. Owners told other owners, and the portfolio grew one property at a time to more than 1,100 units across the Memphis area today.
