How long does a roof last in Memphis?

Architectural asphalt shingles typically last 20 to 25 years here, a bit less than the label says, because Mid-South summer heat and humidity bake shingles and wear off their granules faster. Older 3-tab shingles often go 15 to 20 years. Metal roofs last 40 to 70 years, and TPO flat roofs around 20 to 30.

Signs it's close to the end: curling or cracked shingles, bare spots, granules piling up in the gutters, or daylight through the attic decking. Try the roof age checker for a quick estimate.

Do I need a new roof, or just a repair?

Often a repair. Leaks usually come from one failed spot, like a pipe boot, a flashing seam or wind-lifted shingles. If the rest of the roof is sound, we'll recommend fixing that spot and tell you roughly how many years you have left. Replacement makes sense when damage is widespread, when the roof is near the end of its life, or when insurance is covering storm damage.

Will my homeowners insurance pay for a new roof?

If hail or wind damaged it, often yes. Insurance covers sudden storm damage, not normal aging. Our inspection documents storm damage with dated photos so you can decide whether a claim is worth filing before you call your insurer.

Most policies have a deadline for reporting storm damage, often a year from the storm, so don't wait. Check your policy for the exact window.

Do you help with the insurance claim?

Yes. We meet your adjuster on the roof, point out the damage, and send itemized documentation if something was missed. You stay in charge of your claim, and you pay your deductible. Any roofer who offers to "cover" or "waive" your deductible is asking you to misrepresent the claim, and we won't do it.

Do I need a permit to replace my roof?

In most cases, yes. Re-roofing in Memphis and unincorporated Shelby County is permitted through Shelby County Code Enforcement, and suburbs like Bartlett, Germantown and Collierville have their own codes offices. We pull the permit when one's required and schedule any inspection. It's included in your price.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes are torn off and re-roofed in one day; larger or steeper roofs take two. Scheduling usually runs one to three weeks after you sign, longer during busy stretches after a big storm. We'll give you a date when you approve the quote and call if weather moves it.

What does the free inspection cost, and is there any obligation?

It's free, with no obligation. You keep the photo report whether you hire us or not. Plenty of our inspections end with "your roof is fine, check back in a couple of years."

What warranty do I get?

Two layers: the shingle manufacturer's material warranty, plus our own written workmanship warranty covering installation, which is how leaks from a new roof almost always start. Both are spelled out in your contract.
