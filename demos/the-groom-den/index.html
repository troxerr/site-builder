How it works

Four easy steps. Zero car rides.

You stay home. Your dog stays calm. We handle the rest.

  1. Request a time

    Send the form below or call. Tell us about your dog and the day that suits you.

  2. We confirm

    We text you back with a quote and an arrival window.

  3. We arrive

    The van parks in your driveway. Your dog walks a few steps, not across town.

  4. Happy dog

    Fresh, fluffy and relaxed, handed right back to you at your door.
