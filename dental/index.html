How it works

One job: turn a missed call into an appointment.

It doesn't answer insurance questions or give clinical advice. It books, and it hands everything else to your team.

  1. Step 1

    A call goes unanswered

    Your team is with a patient, at lunch or closed for the day. The caller gets a text instead of voicemail.

  2. Step 2

    It finds out what they need

    New or existing patient, the kind of visit, and the days and times that suit them.

  3. Step 3

    It offers real openings

    Only times that are open in your schedule, then it holds the one they pick while it gets their name and email.

  4. Step 4

    It books and tells your team

    The appointment goes on your calendar, the patient gets a confirmation and reminder, and your front desk gets a summary.

FAQ

Questions practices ask me.

Don't see yours? Call or text or send a message.

Will patients know they're texting an automated assistant?

Yes. The first text says it's your office's automated booking assistant, and patients can reply STAFF at any point to reach a person.

What happens when someone has an emergency?

It doesn't try to book it. Pain, swelling, bleeding or a broken tooth sends your front desk an urgent alert to call the patient back, and the patient is told someone will call shortly. If they mention trouble breathing or swallowing, they're told to call 911 or go to the ER.

Does it work with our practice software?

It works with most practice calendars. Tell me what you use and I'll confirm it connects before you pay anything. If it can't, I'll say so.

Do we need a new website?

No. The agent works with the phone number and calendar you already have. If you do want a new site too, that's priced separately on the main pricing page.

What does it cost, and is there a contract?

$1,500 to set up and $1,000 a month after that, confirmed in writing before any work starts. There's no long-term contract.

Can we see it before we commit?

Yes. Watch the demo for a full conversation, including an urgent request handed to a person. Then send the form below and I'll walk you through how it would work with your phones and calendar.
