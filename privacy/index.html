Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Who we are

Bartlett Website Design provides website design and missed-call text-back services for local businesses. This policy explains how we handle information collected through our website, phone number, and SMS messages.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, business name, the content of your message or inquiry, and basic technical information such as your browser type and pages visited.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, provide a requested draft or quote, deliver missed-call text-back messages, forward your replies to the business owner, maintain records, and improve our services. We do not use your mobile number for unrelated marketing.

SMS messaging

SMS is optional. Our inquiry form has a separate unchecked checkbox and an optional mobile-number field. The form submission includes your choice, number, disclosure text and version, source page, and (when JavaScript is available) a browser-generated consent timestamp. Our form processor delivers the submission to our business inbox with its receipt time. We review and retain this evidence before authorizing automated SMS; the form does not automatically enroll a number into n8n. A call alone is not consent. The program is limited to one missed-call follow-up about your website-design inquiry, with further service replies only in response to your messages. It does not cover contractor intake or automated estimate reminders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

We do not share, sell, rent, or provide your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used only to deliver the messaging program you requested and to support that conversation.

Sharing and service providers

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business, such as our website host, form processor, and SMS provider (Twilio), solely to deliver the services you request. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry and messaging records only as long as needed to respond, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing bartlett.website.design@gmail.com. We may need to retain certain records for legal or operational reasons.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be posted on this page.

Questions? Email bartlett.website.design@gmail.com or call or text (901) 403-7582.
