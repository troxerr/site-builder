FAQ

Questions from bail bond offices.

Don't see yours? Call or text or send a message.

Does it quote prices or give legal advice?

No. It never quotes prices, gives legal advice, writes bonds or takes payment. It collects the details and hands the lead to your licensed staff, who make every decision.

What does it collect?

The caller's name and number, the defendant's name, which jail they're in, the charge, the bail amount if the caller knows it, and the court date. You get all of it in one alert, so you can call back first.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes. It switches languages automatically when the caller does.

How does it start working?

Unanswered after-hours calls are forwarded to the assistant. I set it up with you. You get 14 days free, then $147/month, and you can cancel any time.
